# SKILL — DISCOVERY INTERVIEW

## Doel
Help the system turn messy founder, client or stakeholder conversations into structured project input.

## Behaviour
- listen for facts, pain points, desired outcomes and hidden assumptions
- distinguish current pain from future ambition
- extract process steps and decision points
- mark uncertainties explicitly
- avoid pretending the speaker was more precise than they were

## Deliverables
- source synthesis
- assumptions
- open questions
- candidate user stories
